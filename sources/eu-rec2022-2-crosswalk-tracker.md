## Annex crosswalk: topic clusters to look for in national law

### Annex 1 — model national legislative framework

| Topic cluster | Typical annex articles | What to look for in national legislation / regulation |
|---|---|---|
| National governance and coordination | Arts. 5–7 | National safety/security/service committee, regional/local coordination, national strategy, allocation of responsibilities across ministries and agencies |
| Safety philosophy and venue responsibility | Arts. 8–10, 13 | Integrated safety approach, defined venue/operator responsibilities, duties of safety officers, safety regulations |
| Safety certification and licensing | Arts. 11–20 | Venue safety certificates, certifying authority, inspection powers, enforcement notices, offences and sanctions |
| Venue infrastructure and crowd control | Arts. 21–24, 31–33 | Seating, segregation, CCTV, parking, access routes, operational manuals, communication with police/emergency services |
| Emergency and disaster planning | Arts. 25–29, 35 | Emergency plans, contingency plans, evacuation procedures, exercises/testing, command-and-control arrangements |
| Venue safety service and stewarding | Arts. 34–37 | Safety service, safety officer, stewarding roles, volunteer support, staffing numbers, briefing and training |
| Access control and inclusion | Arts. 38–47 | Ticketing, ingress conditions, prohibited items, searches/inspections, expulsion/refusal of entry, disability access |
| Policing, risk management and exclusion | Arts. 48–68 | Police coordination, risk supporter categorisation, banning orders, databases, National Football Information Point (NFIP/NSIP), information exchange |
| Service, dialogue and inclusion | Arts. 69–80 | Supporter dialogue strategy, supporters’ charters, supporter liaison officers, disability access officers, anti-discrimination, community engagement, communication strategy, fan embassies, fan projects |
| Criminal offences / sanctions examples | Appendix | Ticket fraud, disorder, missile throwing, pitch invasion, assaults, repeat-offender provisions, banning orders, community work |

### Annex 2 — stewarding regulations

| Topic cluster | Typical annex articles | What to look for in national legislation / regulation |
|---|---|---|
| Steward status and qualifications | Arts. 1, 6, 8, 11 | Licensing/qualification requirement, recruitment rules, vetting/background checks, language and conduct standards |
| Steward duties and powers | Arts. 2–4, 9 | Crowd control, search, access control, incident response, first aid, emergency support, liaison with police/supporters |
| Steward code of conduct and visibility | Arts. 5, 12 | Conduct rules, uniform/identification, neutrality, professional behaviour, unique ID numbers |
| Stewarding records and supervision | Arts. 13, 15 | Safety handbooks, training records, event logs, inspection-ready documentation |
| Training and exercises | Arts. 8, 10, 14 | Initial training, refresher training, emergency/evacuation exercises, counter-terrorism awareness, fire safety, BLS/first aid |
| Special support functions | Arts. 3, 4, 7, 16 | Supporter-facing role, customer care, inclusive service, disabled supporter support, applicability of broader private-security law |

- Official football, ministry, or legislation repositories remain the most likely places to find a direct citation or an equivalent national rule, so the matrix should stay open for further verification rather than inference.
